# Overnight expansion: more videos for shows already in ShKids

19 proposed cards with **296 pinned video IDs**. Every ID passed the oEmbed check (200), is unique across cards and is not already in the app's pinned lists. The two exceptions are deliberate: `shmuel-kunda-stories` and `shazak-out-of-egypt` re-include a few IDs the app already has, so each card plays as a complete set in order. Thumbnail grids were checked by eye for every fan-upload group. Machine-readable output is in `expand.json`.

## Flags to review
- **Female voices:** none of the cards are built around women singing. Young girls appear or sing along in some Uncle Moishy DVDs and Mitzvah Blvd segments. Oorah's When Zaidy Was Young has a Bubby puppet. Shazak "Brave Miriam" and "Midwives" may have female speakers. The animated MMM originals are unverified.
- **Audio over a still image:** the last 10 YBC items, several MBC items, most Kinderlach items, the Kunda/Chana song clips, the MMM "Just SONGS" track and the Uncle Moishy Shabbos Medley.
- **Long:** Twins DVDs (45-65 min), the MMM full compilation (2h27), and the Itche Tzivos Hashem rallies (54 min and 1h43).

## Excluded (examples)
- Promos, trailers and ads: Uncle Moishy trailers, Toveedo trailers, Oorah auction/Oorahthon/carnival, Twins sponsor spots, Itche raffle and Patreon promos.
- Adult content: Shmideo satire ("Dumbest Scientist", Thanksgivukkah), Oorah adult sketches (Pesach Blues, Don't Take Kids to a Store), Country Yossi parodies, "Fiveish" critique and reaction videos.
- Duplicates and poor copies: the second copies of Uncle Moishy vol 2 and vol 12, and the Chana clips showing a paused player or a comments panel.
- Unrelated or non-Orthodox material: a 50-min "Mitzvah Boulevard" upload that is unrelated content, Christian "Torah Tots" lookalikes (House of Bread, Ancient Path Kids), a fan-made "New Adventures of MMM", live-band covers, and Aviva Goldfuss reading Boruch books on camera.

About 540 YouTube requests were used (searches, playlist/channel pages and oEmbed checks), with 1.4 s spacing and no throttling encountered.
